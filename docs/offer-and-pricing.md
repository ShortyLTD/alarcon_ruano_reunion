# Offer, discovery, and pricing decisions

Updated September 6, 2026. Published prices are market references, not demonstrated willingness to pay for this product.

Test **$39 once per reunion** when paid checkout and delivery are ready. Keep the working preview free. Test $49 after real buyers explain which local preparation they value. Do not imply $99 is justified simply because the organizer's event is expensive. Do not claim 100 hours of research or time saved without an auditable research log and customer evidence.

## Buyer and promise

The family member responsible for organizing a Santa Cruz reunion wants suitable places, useful provider replies, and clear information for relatives. Sell local groundwork plus the documents that move plans forward. The founder story comes from Jason's account of organizing a reunion for about 100 relatives, not third-party customer proof. All vendors are described as researched, not personally vetted. Capacity must name the room/layout; public menu references are not all-in group quotes. Public beaches are not promised exclusive use.

## Design directions considered

1. **Coastal field guide — selected.** Clear offer and practical sample beside an authentic Santa Cruz photograph and a tangible planning document. Georgia editorial headings, forest ink, warm paper and restrained sage. Desktop split hero; mobile value and CTA first, then smaller image/document composition. This makes local knowledge and deliverables visible. Risk: long page; mitigate with sample and pricing anchors.
2. **Concierge interview.** A large first question, immediate conversational personalization, spare sans typography and short transitions. Fast activation, but new visitors would again have to work before understanding the product. Desktop centered question; mobile full-width input and next action.
3. **Founder-led weekend story.** Photographic case study with timeline and outcomes, warm serif typography and a human story before tools. Stronger when original reunion photos and substantiated booking records are available. Without those materials it would place too much weight on unproven story details. Desktop story mosaic; mobile sequenced moments.

## Selected visual system

Georgia headings: desktop hero 64px, section 44px, mobile hero39–57px, section31–34px. Arial body13–17px. Forest #173f38, paper #f8f6ef, body #53675f, sage #edf1e8. 1180px content grid, generous 53–88px section spacing; 16–20px mobile gutters. 6–11px corners, light borders, one document shadow. No stock customer portraits, invented reviews, countdowns or artificial scarcity. Full-width mobile CTAs and native expandable FAQs. Server-rendered marketing and guides; small client sample component; existing workspace preserved at /plan.

## Research behind the funnel

[Nielsen Norman Group homepage principles](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/homepage-design-principles/) support a clear purpose, representative examples and descriptive actions. This is usability guidance, not a guarantee of a conversion lift.

[Google helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) supports useful original experience, sourcing and clear authorship. Public marketing and guide routes now permit indexing; planner remains noindex and crawlable per [Google indexing guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing). Indexability is not a ranking or traffic guarantee.

## Initial acquisition and validation sequence

1. Publish useful local guides for venues, group dining and hotel blocks. Each should answer the query and show official-source research before a planner CTA.
2. Connect the verified commercial domain and Search Console; submit /sitemap.xml. The original family website has not been modified. Do not create a false local storefront listing for a digital kit.
3. Put the sample in front of five real Santa Cruz family-reunion organizers. Observe what they can complete and what they still need. Ask them to describe the product before explaining it.
4. Connect payment, entitlements, receipts, support and clear purchase terms before collecting money. Use one complete $39 offer first.
5. Measure visits → preview starts → plans built → inquiries opened → downloads → real purchases/refunds. A download is not a sale. Do not claim maximum conversion or increased conversion without observed data.
6. After meaningful paid demand, test $49 against the same offer and acquisition source. Judge net revenue per qualified visitor together with refunds and buyer satisfaction, not conversion alone. Low traffic does not support a confident split-test winner.
7. Prioritize relevant local accommodation/tourism/planning referrals. Draft partner outreach only after operator positioning is agreed; no messages have been sent.

## Remaining release boundaries

No checkout, hosted guest pages, server RSVP store, shared accounts, live quote retrieval, automatic provider email, automated follow-up, or confirmed bookings. Research is an official-source snapshot checked September 6, 2026. The kit is a self-service planning product. Competitor changes are already monitored by the existing weekly task.
